**Effective 28 October 2026.**

Bayfarer is a nautical chart app for iPhone. It collects no data about you.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your location.** Bayfarer uses your iPhone's location to show your boat on the chart, to give speed and course, and to record tracks when you ask it to. Your location is used on your iPhone only and is never sent anywhere.
- **Tracks, waypoints and settings.** They're stored on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, they're included in that backup, under your own Apple account. Bayfarer has no server and no account.
- **Charts** you download are stored on your iPhone and left out of backups, since they can be downloaded again.

## What Bayfarer connects to

- **NOAA** (`charts.noaa.gov`), to download charts and to check for chart updates. These are ordinary web requests: like any website, NOAA's servers see your IP address and which charts are requested. Nothing else is sent.
- **Apple Maps**, for the base map and satellite imagery under the chart, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

That's all. Bayfarer contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.

## Feedback and beta testing

- If you email feedback, I receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.
- If you test Bayfarer through TestFlight, Apple shares with the developer the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send, and your name and email as a tester ([Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers TestFlight).

## Children

Bayfarer isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
