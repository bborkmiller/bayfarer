Bayfarer shows NOAA's official nautical charts on your iPhone, with your boat's position, speed and course, track recording and waypoints. Charts are downloaded directly from NOAA for the waters you sail, and work offline.

Bayfarer is an aid to navigation. It is not a certified ECDIS and does not replace official charts, Notices to Mariners or a proper lookout.

## Support

Questions, problems and suggestions: [bayfarer@breadandsoup.net](mailto:bayfarer@breadandsoup.net). If you're testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in Bayfarer and tap Share Beta Feedback.

[Privacy Policy](privacy): Bayfarer collects no data.
